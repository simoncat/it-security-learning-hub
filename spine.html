Policy and System Lifecycle Overview

Three layers. Layer 1 is why controls exist. Layer 2 is which document binds. Layer 3 is where you are in the life of a system. Each control opens a decision aid or a stage pack.

Layer 1 — Information System Security Objective (CIA)

Confidentiality

Only authorised persons and information systems are allowed to know or gain access to the information stored or processed by information systems in any aspect.

Open the decision aid

Integrity

Only authorised persons and information systems are allowed to make changes to the information stored or processed by information systems in any aspect.

Open the decision aid

Availability (service availability)

Information System is accessible and usable upon demand by authorised persons and information systems.

Open the decision aid
