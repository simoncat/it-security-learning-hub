Placeholder enquiry pack for both. S17 §18 · G3 §18.

Derived from departmental enquiry records (anonymised). The IT Security Unit replaces these pairs with real records.

I clicked a suspicious link but nothing obvious happened. Do I wait to see if I am sure?

No. Report when harm is reasonably possible. Tell your line and the departmental incident contact, and say what you clicked. Do not wait for proof.

Should I post the suspicious message in the team chat so others can check it?

No. Keep the detail to the incident route. Wide sharing spreads the lure and any data in it. Your incident contact will say what others need to know.

May I delete the mail and reimage the PC before I call anyone, so the desk looks tidy?

Do not destroy the timeline before the incident route has told you to. Report first. Preserve what the local procedure says to preserve.
