Placeholder enquiry pack for current staff. S17 §17 · G3 §17.

The supplier says they are certified, so do we still have to write security into the contract?

Yes. Certification does not move the B/D’s Shall items onto the supplier. The contract still has to say what they do, what you still do, and how incidents are reported.

May the vendor keep a copy of our data after the contract ends, for their own analytics?

Not unless the agreement and the information owner allow that specific use. End of contract is when return or destruction is confirmed, not assumed.

Should a cloud design still name our administrator and our logs?

Yes. Shared responsibility still leaves the B/D with access control, oversight, and incident reporting. Write the split before go-live.
