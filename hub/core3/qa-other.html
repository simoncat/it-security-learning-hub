    Our team habit is looser than the departmental procedure. Which one wins?

    The departmental procedure wins over a team habit, and S17 wins where the local procedure is silent or weaker. A local rule may be stricter. It may not weaken a Shall.

    A line says Should. Can we ignore it because the audit sample did not ask?

    No. Should means implement whenever possible. An auditor not asking this year is not a reason to drop it. If you defer it, write why.
