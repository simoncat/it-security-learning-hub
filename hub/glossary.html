    Glossary

    Short meanings used across this hub. Authoritative wording stays in S17, G3, the Security Regulations, and your B/D policy. Source search also treats listed abbreviations and their full names as the same query.

    Shall, Should, May, and Appendix C

    TermMeaning in this hub
    ShallMandatory. You must do it. Keep records that show the control is met.
    ShouldBest practice. Implement whenever possible. If you defer it, know why.
    MayDesirable best practice. Useful guidance, not a substitute for Shall.
    Appendix C / App CExtra controls for Tier 2 and Tier 3 systems. Tier 3 includes every Tier 2 Appendix C control. Baseline S17 and G3 still apply underneath.

    Policy stack

    TermMeaning in this hub
    SR / Security RegulationsGovernment-wide security rules that sit above S17 and G3 for classified protection and related duties.
    S17 / Baseline IT Security PolicyThe baseline policy for HKSARG information systems. Version in this hub: v8.2.
    G3 / IT Security GuidelinesImplementation guidance that pairs with S17 section by section. Version in this hub: v10.2.
    Practice guidesTheme guidance that supports S17 and G3. Use the guide your B/D names; do not invent a control from a guide alone.
    Departmental policyLocal B/D rules and procedures. They may be stricter. They do not weaken a Shall.

    CIA and system tiers

    TermMeaning in this hub
    ConfidentialityOnly authorised persons and systems may know or gain access to the information.
    IntegrityOnly authorised persons and systems may change the information, configuration, or code, and unauthorised change is detectable.
    AvailabilityService availability: authorised users can use the service when they need it. Backup, capacity, continuity, and contingency support that outcome.
    Tier 1Ordinary baseline. S17 and G3 apply. Appendix C does not.
    Tier 2A system whose failure or disruption would seriously affect government operations, or could cause public turmoil or catastrophe. Baseline plus Appendix C.
    Tier 3A Tier 2 system tied directly to an essential service. Takes every Tier 2 Appendix C control, then the Tier 3-only rows.
