Assumed if the PDFs were not re-checked for this build. This page lists the areas. It does not invent clause numbers inside the appendix.

Tier 3 includes Tier 2. Every Tier 2 Appendix C control also applies to Tier 3.

The official appendix text stays in the G3 PDF. The matrix is a learning map of those areas. Confirm a row against the PDF before you quote it.

Appendix C learning map. Tier 3 inherits every Tier 2 control.
AreaTier 2 (also required for Tier 3)Tier 3 only
Organisation (G3 §5)—A steering committee with senior management and the DITSO, meeting regularly, with outcomes and roles recorded. At least one IT security management unit member holds a recognised certification.
Risk (G3 §7)—Use the IT security risk management framework. Keep risk registers that record the risk, likelihood, severity, mitigation, and monitoring.
Human resources (G3 §9)—A structured training programme for staff. Include vendors, contractors, and service providers where feasible, or put the training duty in the contract.
Access (G3 §11)An independent privileged-account check at least every six months. Administrative procedures if technology cannot limit privileged data access. Strong password policy, including on systems that could affect the Tier 2 system. MFA for interactive privileged logon where feasible.—
Operations (G3 §14)Local and off-site backup, stored securely away from the equipment. A documented capacity plan. An end-of-support migration plan at least six months ahead. Patch known vulnerabilities as soon as possible, typically within a month; if not, tell the DITSO and update monthly until fixed.24×7 security monitoring that correlates multiple sources, of a SIEM class, including continuous detection and response.
Development (G3 §16)Shift-left, including secure coding and design-stage security reviews. The pre-production security risk assessment checks that review follow-ups were done. Harden before production and use that build as the baseline.—
Continuity (G3 §19)A documented IT contingency plan for disaster or emergency, tested and tied to the business continuity plan.Enough resilience to avoid disruption of the essential service, tested so failover works.
Compliance (G3 §20)Vulnerability scanning at least yearly, before production, and before major change. Penetration testing inside the security risk assessment. Internet-facing Tier 2 systems: penetration testing at least yearly.A security risk assessment at least annually and before production or major change, including vulnerability scanning, an independent accredited penetration test, and configuration and source review. The DITSO endorses it.
