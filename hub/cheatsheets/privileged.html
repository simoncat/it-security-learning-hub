Privileged accounts and patch timing

Source pointers: G3 §11 · G3 §14 · Appendix C §11 and §14

One production system. One page. Fill the blanks in pencil or in your own note. Do not paste passwords here.

Privileged accounts

  • System name and tier proposal (confirmed tier, if you have it).
  • Inventory: account, purpose, named holder, where the password or key is held, last review date.
  • Least privilege still applies. Shared admin accounts need a written reason and a control.
  • Strong password policy. For Tier 2/3, MFA on interactive privileged logon where it is feasible.
  • If technology cannot limit what a privileged account can read, use an administrative procedure such as split knowledge or a sealed envelope held by someone else.
  • Independent check of privileged use at least every six months for Tier 2 and Tier 3.

Patch and end of support

  • Known vulnerabilities are fixed as soon as possible, typically within a month of the patch.
  • If you cannot, document the risk view and tell the DITSO: why, the risk, and the schedule. Update monthly until it is done.
  • End of support: a migration plan at least six months before the date, and the security measures in place by that date.
  • Tier 3 also needs 24×7 monitoring of a SIEM class. That is not a patch substitute.
Artefacts to keep for yourself: the inventory, the last independent-review date, open patch exceptions, and end-of-support dates. This page does not store them.

Learning aid only. Authoritative text remains S17, G3, Security Regulations, and B/D local policies.
