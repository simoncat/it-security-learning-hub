    Tier 1, 2, and 3

    Source pointers: G3 §4.1 · G3 §7.2 · Appendix B · Appendix C

    TierPlain senseAppendix C
    1An ordinary information system: hardware and software that collect, process, store, communicate, or dispose of information.Baseline S17/G3. No Appendix C.
    2Crucial to government or society. Failure or disruption would seriously affect operations, or could cause public turmoil or catastrophe.All Tier 2 Appendix C controls.
    3A Tier 2 system directly related to an essential service, where disruption could seriously harm the economy, people’s livelihood, or public safety.Every Tier 2 App C control, plus the Tier 3-only rows.

    When you propose a tier

    • Look at data classification and what a disruption would do.
    • Include defence and safety, financial effect, government image, and interdependency.
    • Also look at how many persons are hit, how bad it is, and how long you can be down.
    • G3 Appendix B is guidance for the assessment. It is not a self-service stamp.
    • Do not self-finalise Tier 2 or Tier 3.
    Trigger for Appendix C: Tier 2 or Tier 3. Inheritance: Tier 3 includes Tier 2. Verify the row in the official Appendix C before you brief management.

    Learning aid only. Authoritative text remains S17, G3, Security Regulations, and B/D local policies.
