CIA, risk, and security-by-design

Source pointers: S17 §6 · G3 §6

Write one sentence for each objective on a system you actually use.

ObjectiveIn plain wordsA question to ask
ConfidentialityOnly authorised persons and systems can learn the information.Who can read this, and who just did?
IntegrityOnly authorised persons and systems can change it.Who can alter a record, and would we notice?
AvailabilityAuthorised users can use the system when they need it.What happens if it is down for an hour, a day, a week?

Risk-based

  • Assess impact and likelihood. Treat the risk. Someone owns what remains.
  • Criticality looks at harm to persons and order, money, government image, and knock-on effects between systems.
  • The tier follows that judgement. You propose. The Information Owner or DITSO unit confirms Tier 2 or Tier 3.

Security-by-design

Put security requirements into every phase of the life cycle. Late testing is not a substitute for an early requirement.

Other principle names to recognise

  • Prevent, detect, respond, recover.
  • Protect information while it is processed, in transit, and in storage.
  • Assume an external system is insecure until you have a reason not to.
  • Resilience for crucial systems.
  • Auditability and accountability.
  • Continual improvement.

Learning aid only. Authoritative text remains S17, G3, Security Regulations, and B/D local policies.
