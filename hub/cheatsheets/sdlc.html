    Nine-stage security checklist

    Source pointers: S17 §7 · S17 §16 · S17 §17 · S17 §14 · S17 §18 · S17 §20 · S17 §10

    One line per stage. Open the stage pack for the checklist. Shall first. Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    StageAsk this first
    01 Project initiationWho owns it, what information, what tier intent?
    02 DesignWhere do access, cryptography, and logging live?
    03 ProcurementWhat does the supplier still owe, in the contract?
    04 Build/ImplementationDoes the build match the design, without shared admin passwords?
    05 TestingDoes a Shall fail a test, including the negative case?
    06 Go-liveAre open Shall gaps owned, and is the incident route known?
    07 OperationsAre backup, patch, access review, and reporting actually running?
    08 ChangeDoes this change reopen requirements, tier, or assessment?
    09 DecommissioningAre access, media, and supplier copies closed?
    Not every word of S17 and G3. Deep-read the Shall line for the stage you are in, then the same-numbered G3 section.

    Learning aid only. Authoritative text remains S17, G3, Security Regulations, and B/D local policies.
