    Physical and environmental security

    Secure areas and equipment controls reduce theft, peeking, and casual misuse. There is no Appendix C block on §13.

    Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

    Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    ShallDEC-PHYS-01

    Does physical security apply to equipment I use?

    Yes. Equipment that holds government information is protected against theft and casual viewing, including in an office you think of as safe.

    Relevance
    All staff who have a screen, a laptop, or a server room badge.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You can point at the equipment.
    Implementation guidance
    Follow the local secure-area rules. Visitors and unattended equipment are part of S17 §13 and G3 §13, not a separate topic.
    Source
    S17 §13 G3 §13

    ShouldDEC-PHYS-02

    Do clear desk and clear screen apply to me?

    Yes. Lock the screen when you leave it. Put classified material away rather than leaving it in view or in an open pile.

    Relevance
    Every person with a desk or a laptop.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles, especially elevated users.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You handle information that is not public.
    Implementation guidance
    Make the lock automatic if the desktop policy allows. Clear desk is a habit plus the local rule, not a poster.
    Source
    S17 §13 G3 §13
