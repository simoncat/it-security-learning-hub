    Is this an incident, and who do I tell?

    Report when harm to confidentiality, integrity, availability, or operations is reasonably apparent. Do not wait until you are certain.

    Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

    Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    ShallDEC-IR-01

    Is this something I should report?

    Report when you reasonably see harm, or a real chance of harm, to confidentiality, integrity, service availability, or operations. Certainty is not the threshold.

    Relevance
    Every staff member.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You can say what you saw, when, and which system. Unknown is an acceptable fact.
    Implementation guidance
    Tell your line manager and the departmental incident contact named in the reporting procedure. Follow that procedure. Open S17 §18 and G3 §18 for the duty, not for a script you invent.
    Source
    S17 §18 G3 §18

    ShallDEC-IR-02

    Who do I call first?

    Your departmental reporting route: the line manager and the ISIRT or security-unit contact named in the reporting procedure. Once that team is aware, central reporting clocks belong to them. Do not phone around at random.

    Relevance
    The person who found the problem.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    The reporting procedure should contain comprehensive contact information, such as telephone numbers (office hours, non-office hours and mobile), email addresses, and fax numbers.
    Implementation guidance
    Open the departmental reporting procedure and use the named point of contact. Follow the defined steps. Keep the first report short: what happened, when, and which system. Disclose incident details only on a need-to-know basis. Outside office hours, use the non-office-hour contacts in the procedure. Open G3 §18.
    Source
    S17 §18 G3 §18 S17 §5

    ShouldDEC-IR-03

    What do I preserve?

    Preserve what the local procedure tells you to preserve: what happened, when, which system, and what you already changed. Do not clean up in a way that destroys the timeline.

    Relevance
    The person on the scene, then the incident team.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles. Administrators preserve technical detail. Others preserve the story and the artefacts they hold.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You have reported, or you are reporting now. Preservation does not wait for a perfect write-up.
    Implementation guidance
    Follow G3 §18 and the local incident procedure. Keep passwords and unnecessary personal data out of your note.
    Source
    S17 §18 G3 §18
