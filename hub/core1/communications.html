    ShallDEC-COMM-01

    May I send this information on this channel?

    Only if the channel is allowed for that classification. Mail, messaging, and file transfer do not become safe because they are convenient.

    Relevance
    Every person who sends government information, and administrators who provide the channel.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You know the classification, or you have stopped to ask.
    Implementation guidance
    Use the approved channel and the required marking. Open S17 §15 and G3 §15 if the local procedure is unclear, then ask the security unit rather than guessing.
    Source
    S17 §15 G3 §15 S17 §10

    ShallDEC-COMM-02

    Does an Internet-facing or wireless service need extra care?

    Yes. Segmentation, gateways, and wireless are part of communications security. Exposure is designed, not discovered after go-live.

    Relevance
    Administrators and developers of network services.
    Applicability
    Roles: System / LAN administrator and Application developer.Stages: 02 Design, 04 Build/Implementation, 07 Operations.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You can draw where the service sits relative to the Internet and to internal users.
    Implementation guidance
    Put the control in the design: gateway, segmentation, and the administrative path. Pair with access control for admin logon.
    Source
    S17 §15 G3 §15 S17 §11
