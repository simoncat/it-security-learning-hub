Asset management

Know the asset, who owns it, how the information is classified, and how it is returned or destroyed.

Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

ShallDEC-ASSET-01

Does this asset need an owner?

Yes. Information and the systems that hold it need an owner so classification, access, and disposal have someone to decide.

Relevance
Administrators and project staff adding a system, share, or dataset.
Applicability
Roles: All roles that create or run assets.Stages: 01 Project initiation, 04 Build/Implementation, 07 Operations, 09 Decommissioning.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You can describe the asset. An owner is a role, not a shared mailbox with no person.
Implementation guidance
Record the owner in the inventory your B/D uses. If the inventory has no row, the missing row is the gap.
Source
S17 §10 G3 §10

ShallDEC-ASSET-02

Do I label this before I send or store it?

Classify and label information so the handler knows the protection. An unlabelled extract is how classified information walks out in ordinary mail.

Relevance
Anyone who copies data into mail, a drive, or a ticket.
Applicability
Roles: All roles, especially elevated users.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You know the classification rules your B/D uses under the Security Regulations. This hub does not restate those labels.
Implementation guidance
Apply the local classification and labelling rules, then choose a channel that is allowed for that label. See the email Q&A.
Source
S17 §10 G3 §10 S17 §15

ShallDEC-ASSET-03

Someone is leaving, or a disk is being reused. What must happen first?

Assets are returned when the person leaves. Information is erased before media is reused or discarded, to the standard your procedure sets for that classification.

Relevance
Administrators and the persons who collect equipment.
Applicability
Roles: Elevated end user, System / LAN administrator, and IT security administrator / unit.Stages: 07 Operations, 09 Decommissioning.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You have the media or the equipment, and you know its classification. If you do not, stop and ask.
Implementation guidance
Follow the local disposal procedure. Do not rely on a file delete. Record what was sanitised if the procedure asks for a record.
Source
S17 §10 G3 §10
