Which document applies?

Security Regulations, then S17, then G3, then practice guides, then departmental policies and procedures. Local rules may be stricter. They do not weaken a Shall.

Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C only if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3. Open the source pages for the original wording.

ShallDEC-STACK-01

Which document do I open first?

Open S17 for the mandatory minimum (the what). Then open the same-numbered G3 section for the implementation standard (the how).

Relevance
Anyone starting a theme or arguing from the wrong document.
Applicability
Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You know the theme well enough to pick a section, even if you have not read it yet.
Implementation guidance
Use the policy stack on this page, then the source contents. Practice guides and local procedures come after G3, not instead of it.

ShallDEC-STACK-02

Can our departmental procedure be looser than S17?

No. Departmental policies and procedures are applied after S17 and G3. They may be stricter. They shall not undercut a Shall.

Relevance
Staff comparing a local habit with the baseline.
Applicability
Roles: All roles. Local policy owners as well as persons who follow the procedure.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You have the local procedure and the S17 section it is meant to implement.
Implementation guidance
Where the local text is silent or weaker, follow S17 and G3 and tell the policy owner. Record the gap on the gap template if it is a live system.
Source
S17 §2 G3 §2 S17 §8 G3 §8

ShouldDEC-STACK-03

Where do practice guides sit?

Practice guides add implementation detail for topical issues. They support G3. They do not replace S17.

Relevance
Staff who were handed a practice guide and nothing else.
Applicability
Roles: All roles, especially project and operations staff.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You know which S17/G3 section the guide supports.
Implementation guidance
Read the matching S17 and G3 sections, then the practice guide your unit points to. This hub does not copy the guides.
