    Which document applies?

    Security Regulations, then S17, then G3, then practice guides, then departmental policies and procedures. Local rules may be stricter. They do not weaken a Shall.

    Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

    Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    ShallDEC-STACK-01

    What is the relationship of S17 and G3?

    Open S17 for the mandatory minimum (the what). Then open the same-numbered G3 section for the implementation standard (the how).

    Relevance
    For staff starting a theme who need to know how S17 and G3 relate.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You have a basic idea of S17/G3 and you are asked to familiarise yourself with them.
    Implementation guidance
    Use the policy stack on this page, then the source contents. Practice guides and local procedures come after G3, not instead of it.
    Source
    S17 §1 S17 §2 G3 §1 G3 §2

    ShallDEC-STACK-02

    Can our departmental procedure be looser than S17?

    No. Departmental policies and procedures are applied after S17 and G3.

    Relevance
    When staff are considering updating the Departmental IT Security Policy.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles. Owner of the Departmental IT Security Policy as well as persons who follow the policies.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You have the local procedure and the S17 section it is meant to implement.
    Implementation guidance
    Departmental IT Security Policy shall be formulated based on all the security requirements and implementation guidance specified in the Security Regulations and the Government IT Security Policy and Guidelines. B/Ds shall conduct a review of their Departmental IT Security Policies, standards, guidelines periodically.
    Source
    S17 §2 G3 §2 S17 §8 G3 §8

    ShouldDEC-STACK-03

    When should I refer to practice guides?

    Practice guides give technical guidance on specific topic that supplements G3. You may access them via IT Security Theme Page.

    Relevance
    For staff who need deeper technical guidance on a particular control area beyond S17/G3 to address and mitigate risk brought by emerging technologies and security threats.
    Applicability
    Roles: All roles, especially project and operations staff.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
    Prerequisites
    You know which S17/G3 section the guide supports.
    Implementation guidance
    Read the matching S17 and G3 sections, then refer to the practice guide your section points to.
    Source
    S17 §2 G3 §2
