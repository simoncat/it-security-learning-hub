Confidentiality, integrity, and availability

Every control serves confidentiality, integrity, or availability. Availability here means service availability: authorised persons can use the service when they need it.

Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

ShallDEC-CIA-01

Who is allowed to read this information?

If a file of staff personal data is posted to a team channel that includes persons who must not see it, confidentiality has failed. Limit who can read the information, and notice when they do.

Relevance
Anyone handling records, mail, files, or screens.
Applicability
Roles: All roles.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You have defined the stakeholders of the system.
Implementation guidance
For an information system, only authorised users shall be allowed to know or gain access to the information stored or processed by the information system. Data access rights shall be granted to users based on a need-to-know basis. Ensure the least privilege principle is enforced when assigning resources and privileges of information systems to users.
Source
S17 §6 G3 §6

ShallDEC-CIA-02

Who is allowed to change the record or the system setting, and would we notice the change?

Integrity fails when an unauthorised change to data, system configuration or software code occurs, or when an authorised change cannot be detected. Controls include access, logging, cryptography, and verification on records.

Relevance
Developers, administrators, and anyone who has the authority to approve the changes.
Applicability
Roles: All roles.Stages: 02 Design, 04 Build/Implementation, 05 Testing, 07 Operations, 08 Change.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You have defined which kinds of records or system settings must stay trustworthy.
Implementation guidance
For an information system, only authorised users shall be allowed to make changes, or delete the information stored or processed by the information system.
Source
S17 §6 G3 §6

ShallDEC-CIA-03

What happens if the service is down?

Availability, means service availability. Authorised users can use the service when they need it. Backup, capacity, and continuity exist for that outcome.

Relevance
Service owners, operators, and management support.
Applicability
Roles: All roles.Stages: 01 Project initiation, 06 Go-live, 07 Operations, 09 Decommissioning.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You know which service, and roughly how long an outage can be tolerated.
Implementation guidance
Document the service, the tolerance, and where backup, continuity or contingency is described.
Source
S17 §6 G3 §6 S17 §14 G3 §19
