    Management support — one-page management brief

    Source pointers: G3 §5.2 · §7 · §20 · Appendix C

    Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

    Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    Where a step is Should, you may defer it if you record why. Shall steps are the ones the policy treats as mandatory. App C steps apply when the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    1. Step 1. State the audience

      Steering committee, directorate, or DITSO. One audience only.

      Artefacts to keep: Audience line.

    2. Step 2. Draft the tier inventory

      System, proposed or confirmed tier, owner. Mark unconfirmed tiers as proposals.

      Artefacts to keep: The inventory table.

      Shall: do not self-finalise Tier 2 or 3.

    3. Step 3. Name three residual risks

      Each risk has likelihood, severity, and the mitigation already in train. Stop at three.

      Artefacts to keep: Three rows.

    4. Step 4. App C red / amber / green

      For confirmed Tier 2/3 systems, ask the unit for a status on privileged review, patch exceptions, backup, and the latest security risk assessment. You are not collecting packet captures.

      Artefacts to keep: The status the unit gives you.

    5. Step 5. One decision

      The paper asks for one decision: fund or fix the top residual risk. Say what “done” means.

      Artefacts to keep: The decision sentence.

    6. Step 6. Assurance dates

      Next security risk assessment, audit, or Appendix D submission. The owner’s dates, not guesses.

      Artefacts to keep: The dates.

    7. Step 7. Training and committee

      If any system is Tier 3: is the steering committee meeting, and is there a certified security-unit member? Those are App C Shall items.

      Artefacts to keep: Yes, no, or unknown.

    8. Step 8. Keep the paper unofficial until it is issued

      This walkthrough produces a learning draft. It becomes a management paper only through your B/D’s own route.

      Artefacts to keep: The draft, marked draft.

    Alternative exercise

    There is no technical alternate. If you need a drill, ask the unit to walk you through one red item on their status view.
