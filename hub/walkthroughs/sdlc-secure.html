    Secure life-cycle walkthrough

    Source pointers: S17 §16 · G3 §16 · S17 §7 · S17 §20

    Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

    Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    Where a step is Should, you may defer it if you record why. Shall steps are the ones the policy treats as mandatory. App C steps apply when the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

    1. Step 1. Name one change

      A small change you know. If you use a real backlog item, keep secrets and personal data out of the note.

      Artefacts to keep: Change name.

    2. Step 2. Mark the stage

      Say which of the nine stages you are actually in. Do not start at go-live if you are still in design.

      Artefacts to keep: The stage name.

    3. Step 3. Write one Shall requirement

      Tie it to confidentiality, integrity, or service availability. “Make it secure” is not a requirement.

      Artefacts to keep: The requirement sentence.

      Shall: security-by-design.

    4. Step 4. Name the owner and the tier state

      Information owner, and whether the tier is confirmed or only a proposal. Do not finalise the tier yourself.

      Artefacts to keep: Owner and tier state.

    5. Step 5. Add the test

      A test that fails if the requirement is missing, including someone who should not see the record.

      Artefacts to keep: The test case.

    6. Step 6. Supplier line

      If a supplier is involved, one sentence on what they own and what the B/D still owns.

      Artefacts to keep: The split.

      Skip only when no supplier is in the path. Say so.

    7. Step 7. Appendix C question

      If the tier might be 2 or 3, write the Appendix C question you will ask. Do not answer it from memory.

      Artefacts to keep: The question.

      App C once the tier is confirmed. Tier 3 inherits Tier 2.

    8. Step 8. Hand the note to the stage pack

      Open the matching SDLC pack and tick only what you actually did. The tick stays in this browser.

      Artefacts to keep: The pack name.

    Alternative exercise

    If you are not on a project this month, walk an old change that already shipped and mark it as a retrospective.
