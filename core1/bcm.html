Continuity and recovery

Continuity is how service availability survives a disruption. Appendix C adds more for Tier 2 and Tier 3.

Not every-word — S17 first, then G3; deep-read Shall and App C; Should = implement whenever possible.

Reading order: S17, then the same-theme G3 section, then Appendix C if the system is Tier 2 or Tier 3.

ShallDEC-BCM-01

Does continuity planning apply to this service?

If the service matters to the B/D’s work, it needs a planned way to continue or recover. The depth follows the tier and the business need.

Relevance
Service owners and management support.
Applicability
Roles: System / LAN administrator, IT security administrator / unit, and Management support.Stages: 01 Project initiation, 07 Operations.Tiers: Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
You know the service and roughly how long it can be down.
Implementation guidance
Point at the business continuity arrangement and the IT part of it. Open S17 §19 and G3 §19. Backup under §14 is related and is not the whole answer.
Source
S17 §19 G3 §19 S17 §14

Shall App CDEC-BCM-02

What does Appendix C add for Tier 2 and Tier 3?

Tier 2 adds a documented, tested IT contingency tied to the business continuity plan. Tier 3 adds resilience for the essential service, tested so failover works. Tier 3 also keeps the Tier 2 control.

Relevance
Owners of Tier 2 and Tier 3 services.
Applicability
Roles: System / LAN administrator, IT security administrator / unit, and Management support.Stages: Any stage.Tiers: Tier 2, Tier 3.
Prerequisites
The tier is confirmed.
Implementation guidance
Confirm the continuity rows in G3 Appendix C. A document that has never been tested does not meet a testing expectation.
Source
G3 Appendix C S17 §19 G3 §19
