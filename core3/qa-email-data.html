Email, data handling, and classification

Placeholder enquiry pack for both. S17 §10 · S17 §15 · G3 §15.

May I send an extract of internal records to my personal mailbox so I can work at home?

No. Use the channel your procedure allows for that classification. A personal mailbox is not an approved store for government information.

The spreadsheet is not marked. Can I treat it as unclassified and forward it?

No. Find the classification before you send it. An unmarked file is a reason to stop, not a reason to assume it is public.

Should I encrypt a message that leaves the government mail system?

When the classification or the channel requires protection, yes. Use the method your B/D standard names. This hub does not pick an algorithm for you.
